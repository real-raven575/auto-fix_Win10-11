# Auto-Fixer wiki

Auto-Fixer is a free, open-source (MIT) repair tool for Windows 10 and 11. It reads the logs your PC already keeps, tells you in plain English what went wrong, and fixes it only with your OK.

- 🚀 [Getting started](Getting-started)
- 👥 [Modes and settings](Modes-and-settings)
- 🙈 [Privacy and logs](Privacy-and-logs)
- 🩺 [Troubleshooting and FAQ](Troubleshooting-and-FAQ)
- 🗺️ [What's next](Whats-next)

These pages live in the repo under `docs/wiki/`. Change them there, through a pull request; when it merges, the GitHub Wiki tab is updated to match. Edits made on the Wiki tab itself are overwritten.
